Alternates · priced separately

Options the fabricator quotes as adds

  • ALT-1
    Ventilation

    One 2" dia. grommet with cap through the back panel at each bay (12), or routed vent slots per bay. Not needed while laptops are stored cold; useful if they go in warm.

  • ALT-2
    Charging

    Not in scope: the client has not asked for charging. If needed later, quote a UL-listed charging solution separately (power strips inside casework are not code-compliant).

  • ALT-3
    Mobile base

    Replace toe kick and rear rail with a 1" sub-base on 4 x 3" locking casters, keeping 35" overall height.

  • ALT-4
    Bay labels

    Engraved or printed ID plates A1 to D3 on shelf fronts (12), plus A to D on door fronts (4).

  • ALT-5
    Master-keyed locks

    Locks keyed differently per door with one master key, instead of keyed alike.

Open items

To confirm with the client before release

  1. Exact laptop model and size, with or without a protective case.
  2. No charging inside the cabinet is assumed. Confirm; if it changes, see ALT-1 and ALT-2.
  3. Door pull and lock finish: brushed nickel or black.
  4. Is 35" a hard maximum, for example under a window? Glides add about 3/8".
  5. Against a wall, or freestanding with a finished back?
  6. Laminate color, and how many cabinets.
