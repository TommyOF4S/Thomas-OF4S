Alternates · priced separately

Options the fabricator quotes as adds

  • ALT-1
    Lockable doors

    4 full-overlay doors, one per column, 12-1/8" W x 29-7/8" H x 3/4" TFM, 3 mm PVC all edges. 2 concealed 110° soft-close hinges per door (half-overlay on dividers). 1 keyed-alike cam lock per door, 1 wire pull per door (4" c.c.). Adds 3/4" to depth.

  • ALT-2
    Ventilation

    One 2" dia. grommet hole with cap through the back panel at each bay (12), or 3 x 1/2" x 6" routed vent slots per bay. Needed if laptops go in warm.

  • ALT-3
    Charging

    Not in base scope. If laptops must charge inside, quote a UL-listed charging solution separately (power strips inside casework are not code-compliant).

  • ALT-4
    Mobile base

    Replace toe kick and rear rail with a 1" sub-base on 4 x 3" locking casters, keeping 35" overall height.

  • ALT-5
    Bay labels

    Engraved or printed bay ID plates A1 to D3 on shelf fronts, 12 total.

Open items

To confirm with the client before release

  1. Exact laptop model and size, with or without a protective case.
  2. Will laptops charge inside the cabinet? That changes ventilation and power (ALT-2, ALT-3).
  3. Doors and locks, or open bays? (ALT-1)
  4. Is 35" a hard maximum, for example under a window? Glides add about 3/8".
  5. Against a wall, or freestanding with a finished back?
  6. Laminate color, and how many cabinets.
