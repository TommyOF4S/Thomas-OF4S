Best: the CET 2D exported as a PDF with panel labels on. The page measures every panel joint and reads each panel's height from its label. PNG and JPG work too, for pink dashes and colored dots.

Height changes: nothing to draw when the 2D is a PDF with CET's panel labels (A6660 = 66"H × 60"W). Where panels of different heights meet, the connector is quoted at the taller height and a high/low filler strip covers the step, such as HMHL3967 where a 42" (Devon 39") panel meets a 66" (Devon 67").

For a PNG or JPG, or to override the labels, put a colored dot on each joint where the height changes. The color picks the filler strip:
  • Red · 39" to 53" · HMHL3953
  • Blue · 39" to 67" · HMHL3967
  • Yellow · 47" to 53" · HMHL4753
  • Green · 47" to 67" · HMHL4767
  • Purple · 53" to 67" · HMHL5367
Dots win over labels. 39" to 47" (HMHL3947) has no color, but the labels find it.

Panel-to-panel connectors: draw a pink dash on each panel-to-panel joint. When the plan has pink dashes, each marked joint is quoted as one panel-to-panel connector (HMP213), and any straight joint without a dash is flagged. Without pink dashes, the straight joints the page measures are used.

Notes or dictationOptional

To dictate, click in the box and press Win + H on Windows, or press Fn twice on a Mac. The notes go into the review report.
