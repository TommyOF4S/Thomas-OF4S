## Mapping descriptions to models

- All solid: T101 (one piece), T102 to T104 (2 to 4 horizontal segments).
- All glass: T105 framed 1/4", T105F frameless 3/8", T106 to T108 (2 to 4 segments).
- Solid base 32" with glass above: T109, frameless T109F. Glass in 2 or 3 segments: T113, T114.
- Glass to door height (80"), solid above: T110, frameless T110F.
- Solid to 8', glass above: T111.
- Solid base, glass band to door height, solid above: T112, frameless T112F.
- Solid in 2 to 5 segments with glass above: T115 to T118.
- Models 13 to 16 match Teilen's catalog drawings only approximately, and T117 and T118 have no drawing. Say so when you use them.

## Door height rules

- HW1, A1, FL1, HWS1, FLB are 80" tall. Flag a wall shorter than 80".
- A1C up to 96", FLP up to 108", FLHF, FLPS, FLS, FLSS up to 120".
- FLP.5 is for openings 108" to 120". On a shorter wall suggest FLP.
- Each door is 36" wide. Flag a wall too short for its doors.
